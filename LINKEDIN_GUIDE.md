## 📋 Step 3: Create Your LinkedIn Post

### On Desktop:
1. Go to [LinkedIn.com](https://www.linkedin.com)
2. Click "Start a post"
3. Paste your chosen template
4. Customize with your own voice
5. Click the image icon to add screenshots (1-4 images max)
6. Add 5-10 hashtags at the bottom
7. Click "Post"

### On Mobile:
1. Open LinkedIn app
2. Tap "+" to create post
3. Paste template
4. Tap image icon to add photos
5. Post!

---

## 🎨 Post Optimization Tips

### Best Practices:
✅ Post between 7-9 AM or 5-6 PM (peak engagement times)
✅ Use emojis sparingly for visual breaks
✅ Keep paragraphs short (2-3 lines max)
✅ Include line breaks for readability
✅ Add 5-10 relevant hashtags
✅ Include your GitHub link
✅ Ask a question at the end to encourage comments

### What to Include:
- **Hook**: First line should grab attention
- **Problem**: What problem does it solve?
- **Solution**: Your approach
- **Results**: Numbers and metrics (86.58% accuracy!)
- **Tech Stack**: Technologies used
- **Link**: GitHub repository
- **Call to Action**: Ask for feedback/collaboration
